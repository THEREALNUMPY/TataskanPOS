# SukiPOS v1.1 — Release Notes 🚀
**The Reliability & Analytics Update**

This update transforms SukiPOS into a more robust, data-driven tool for your business. We’ve focused on three core pillars: **Visual Analytics**, **Bulletproof Data Safety**, and **Global Accessibility**.

---

### 📊 New: Business Insights & Advanced Reporting
*   **Weekly Sales Trend:** Track your revenue growth with new interactive bar charts showing your performance over the last 7 days.
*   **Top Products Leaderboard:** Identify your bestsellers at a glance with new visual performance indicators and revenue tracking.
*   **Spreadsheet Export (CSV):** Beyond professional PDFs, you can now export your raw sales data to CSV format for advanced accounting in Excel or Google Sheets.

### 🛡️ Enhanced: Data Safety & Backups
*   **Unified Backup System:** Our new `.sukipos` format now saves your **entire profile**—including store settings, logo, and login sessions—alongside your inventory in a single file.
*   **Secure Recovery:** Restore data directly from your local backup list with one tap. 
*   **Wipe Protection:** Added a new safety layer for account deletion; you now confirm your shop name to prevent accidental data loss.
*   **Backup Vigilance:** A new "No Backup Found" warning ensures you never forget to secure your business records.

### 🌍 Improved: Multilingual & Responsive Design
*   **Dynamic UI:** Redesigned layouts to handle long translations (Tagalog, Spanish, French, Hindi, Chinese) perfectly without text cut-off.
*   **Full Localization:** The entire app, including navigation titles and reports, now speaks your language fluently.
*   **Device Compatibility:** Optimized scrolling and button scaling to ensure a professional look on everything from compact phones to large tablets.

### 🛒 UX & Performance Polish
*   **Quick Checkout:** New "Quick Cash" chips in the checkout screen help you calculate change faster than ever.
*   **Security Tweaks:** Added a password visibility toggle during registration for better accuracy.
*   **Visual Refresh:** Added descriptive icons to the dashboard and settings for a more intuitive experience.
*   **Stability:** Fixed a critical issue where data wasn't fully committing to backups before export.
